Enduring Physical and Verbal Challenges
🧘
Q: How do I practice patience with physical discomfort and verbal abuse?

A: Endure physical discomfort (cold, heat, hunger, thirst, insects, pain) and rude criticism without retaliating when abused or argued with, which builds happiness, calmness, and inner peace through deliberate exposure to difficult conditions.

Understanding Life's Nature
🔄
Q: What mental framework makes patience easier to practice?

A: Study the three marks of existence (impermanence, suffering, non-self) to accept that pain, suffering, and difficult situations are inevitable parts of life's transient nature subject to change, allowing you to let go of anger and deal with adversity without worry.
