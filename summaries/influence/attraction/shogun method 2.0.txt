### Chapter 2: The Online IRAE Model—Framework and Adaptations for Online Dating

The cornerstone of any successful seduction strategy detailed in this guide is the **IRAE Model**, which represents a **linear progression** through four sequential stages:

- **Intrigue**: Capturing and holding the target’s attention.
- **Rapport**: Building a connection that deepens the hypnotic trance.
- **Attraction**: Creating intense emotional involvement.
- **Enslavement**: Achieving emotional dependence.

However, the online environment presents unique challenges:

- **Limited Non-Verbal Cues**: Techniques based on *body language*, *anchoring*, or *microsignals* require physical presence and thus cannot be fully utilized online.
- **Handicapped Environment**: Without face-to-face interaction, many advanced stages like **Attraction** and **Enslavement** suffer in efficacy and often cannot be completed.
- **Necessity of Face-to-Face Transition**: The ultimate goal of online interaction is to transition to in-person meetings where the latter IRAE stages can be executed effectively.

Key distinctions in applying the IRAE Model online:

- **Intrigue** can be fully accomplished via online messaging, matching the potency of in-person interaction.
- **Rapport** formation remains viable but slightly less effective due to lack of physical feedback.
- **Attraction and Enslavement** stages require direct interaction and cannot be achieved through messaging alone.

This understanding reframes the **primary end goal** of online dating within the ODP: **build sufficient intrigue and rapport to secure offline meetings**, after which the Shogun Method can be deployed to complete the IRAE sequence.

### Chapter 3: Dangers and Pitfalls in Online Dating

The guide identifies several hazards confronting users of online dating platforms that can derail success if unaddressed:

- **Danger #1: Contextual Rapport**  
  The phenomenon where online interactions foster familiarity based solely on the digital context—not genuine personal connection—leads to being perceived as "just another guy she talks to online," and ultimately result in the **friend zone**. This problem is exacerbated in online dating because the target has little to no mental framework (such as voice or mannerisms) to build upon, making quick judgment more likely.

- **Danger #2: Forced Familiarity**  
  Occurs when participants artificially force connection by agreeing excessively or superficially on interests without depth. This undermines authenticity and damages the prospect of genuine rapport.

- **Danger #3: Intense Competition**  
  Unlike offline environments with limited rivals, online dating platforms expose you to potentially thousands of competitors within your geographical area. This amplifies the challenge to **stand out quickly and efficiently**.

- **Danger #4: Precondition #2 Violations**  
  Precondition #2 from the core Shogun Method stresses the importance of **concealing your intentions** until manipulation is underway. Social media profiles often inadvertently violate this by appearing too overtly focused on seduction, causing targets to raise defenses. Contrastingly, online dating profiles inherently signal dating intent, making intentional concealment harder but manageable. Notably, the first message sent usually violates Precondition #2; however, with proper approach and script usage, success remains attainable despite this.

### Chapter 4: Profile Development Strategy in Online Dating

Successful online seduction requires excelling in two areas: **profile development** and **messaging strategy**.

#### The VCEA Model of Online Dating

Paralleling the IRAE Model, the **VCEA Model** serves as an online-specific roadmap representing four sequential stages:

1. **Visibility** – Your profile’s appearance in search results and user feeds.
2. **Clickthrough** – Potential matches clicking your profile to learn more.
3. **Engagement** – Viewing and reading your profile content.
4. **Action** – The target initiating contact by messaging or responding.

Each stage is essential, and failure at any point disrupts the progression of interaction.

---

#### Visibility Optimization

Common "hacks" to improve profile visibility (e.g., timing updates, paying for promotion, alphabetizing profile titles) exist but hold negligible long-term value due to:

- High availability to all users (zero-sum digital game).
- Frequent changes in platform algorithms.
- The platform’s vested interest in promoting genuine, high-quality profiles.

Therefore, the guide advises focusing on **creating a compelling profile** that naturally ranks higher due to lasting appeal.

---

#### Clickthrough Optimization – Profile Pictures as Intrigue Pings

The most critical factor determining profile clicks is the quality and type of **profile pictures**, conceptualized as **Intrigue Pings**—photos that provoke interest and curiosity.

**Key insights about profile pictures:**

- Women respond most positively to **engaging photographs** showing the subject partaking in interesting activities or settings, rather than:
  - Explicit photos (shirtless, naked), which underperform.
  - Generic face/upper body shots lacking context, which are uninspiring.

- Engaging photos work because they evoke subconscious curiosity, portraying stories rather than superficial appearance, and usually depict one of the following:
  1. Public engagement (speaking on stage, leading).
  2. Adventure or action (mountain climbing, bungee jumping).
  3. Iconic or exotic locations (e.g., Taj Mahal, Eiffel Tower).

- Use a **combination** of three to four photos: one main engaging photo, supplemented by one or two generic shots showing your everyday appearance. This balance intrigues without overexposure.

- **Authenticity is crucial**: Photos must truthfully represent your current appearance to prevent disappointment or distrust, which can sabotage offline meetings.

---

#### Engagement Optimization and Crafting the Perfect Headline

Once visibility is secured, you must **retain attention** with profile text employing **Intrigue Pings** and **Yes Ladders**:

- **Headlines** (or opening lines): Utilize short, provocative questions or statements that directly engage the woman’s subconscious and invite clicks.

  Example headline:
  > “Ask yourself something… are you sick of meeting average guys?”

- Avoid clichés and mundane affirmations (“fun, outgoing guy”), which fail to attract.

- The **Yes Ladder** technique involves a sequence of questions/statements that each prompt a "yes" answer, creating subconscious agreement that culminates in a call to action.

**Example Yes Ladder progression:**

- “Did something about me grab your attention?”
- “Are you searching for a guy you can truly connect with?”
- “Do you want to finally meet someone real, not just online?”
- “Message me if you think you can handle it.”

This progressive affirmation builds trust and nudges the target toward initiating contact.

---

#### Continuous Profile Optimization

Effective online dating relies on rigorous **testing and iteration**. Success stems from:

1. Experimenting with various pictures, headlines, and bio statements.
2. Measuring response rates from potential matches.
3. Refining profile elements accordingly to maximize engagement.

This scientific approach transforms profiles into powerful assets that consistently attract interest across platforms.

### Chapter 5: Online Messaging Strategy—Intrigue and Rapport Building

#### First Contact: Importance of an Intrigue Ping

The opening online message is **critical**; it must generate **Intrigue** immediately to prevent swift rejection.

- Online rejection is easier—women can dismiss without social cost by ignoring or deleting.
- The first message must stand out to avoid instant loss of opportunity.

The formula for online Intrigue Pings follows the **[Statement / Response / Question]** pattern, designed to involve the woman actively, baiting her to respond.

Example:

- You: “Don’t think of me as arrogant, but you’ve got me thinking about something…”
- Her: “What’s that?”
- You: “At what point would you stop what you’re doing and message me?”

This interaction stimulates curiosity and compels participation, indicating you’re worth engagement subconsciously.

---

#### Escalation to Rapport Stage

Escalating from Intrigue to **Rapport** online requires patient, natural conversation without rushing or forcing emotional closeness.

Common mistakes include:

- Pushing too quickly before adequate Intrigue is established.
- Creating **forced familiarity**, harming credibility.
- Overwhelming with too many questions or excessive messaging.

Two adapted techniques facilitate building Rapport digitally:

1. **Multiple Outs**: Phrasing questions/statements that are "correct" regardless of her answer, creating a subconscious impression that you understand her deeply.

   Structures include:

   - “You’re not…[X]…are you?”
   - “It’s not…[X]…is it?”
   - “[X] hasn’t [X]…right?”

   Example:
   > “You’re not looking for anything short-term, are you?”

2. **VAKSOG Mode Matching**: Aligning your communication style with her primary sensory processing mode—**Visual (seeing), Auditory (hearing), Kinesthetic (feeling), Somatosensory (touch), Olfactory (smell), Gustatory (taste)**.

   - Analyze her language to determine dominant VAKSOG channels.
   - Mirror her style to build subconscious rapport.
   - Example: If she uses visual language (“seeing,” “looks like”), respond with vivid imagery.

### Chapter 6: Asking for a Date Using Implanted Commands

Having built Intrigue and Rapport, the next vital step is securing an offline meeting to continue the IRAE process.

Due to online constraints, the **Implanted Commands** technique—usually reserved for the Attraction stage—is adapted for text to subtly suggest and "plant" the idea of a date without explicit directness.

Key adaptability for texting:

- Deliver commands in two parts to obscure intent and reduce conscious resistance.
- Examples of two-part commands:

  > “I don’t really know if you would have the best date of your life with me, but…”  
  > “It’s a challenge I’d be willing to accept.”

- Effective commands rely on prior built agreement to reduce defenses.

Example Implanted Commands:

- “You’ll never know if you’d have an amazing time with me this Friday.”
- “I wonder if you’d agree to meet me this weekend and possibly have the best time of your life.”
- “Some women might refuse my offer at first, but something tells me you’re not going to.”

When deployed correctly, these commands significantly increase the likelihood of an in-person meeting, propelling the IRAE Model toward the Attraction and Enslavement stages.
