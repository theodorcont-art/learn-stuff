CORE MANIPULATION TACTICS
1. FRAMING CONTROL
Technique: Present information in a way that controls perception
Examples:
"95% fat-free" vs. "5% fat"
"Investment opportunity" vs. "financial risk"
"Strategic workforce reduction" vs. "mass layoffs"
Why it works: The brain processes the frame, not just the facts
2. RECIPROCITY TRAP
Technique: Give something small to create obligation for something big
Examples:
Free sample → pressure to buy
Compliment → request for favor
"Free" trial that auto-bills
Why it works: Humans feel compelled to return favors
3. SOCIAL PROOF MANIPULATION
Technique: Use others' behavior to validate your position
Examples:
Fake testimonials
"Everyone's buying this"
Crowded restaurant = good food assumption
Why it works: We assume others know what they're doing
4. AUTHORITY EXPLOITATION
Technique: Use perceived authority to bypass critical thinking
Examples:
Fake credentials
"Studies show..." without citing sources
Dressing the part (doctor's coat, business suit)
Why it works: We automatically defer to perceived experts
5. SCARCITY & URGENCY
Technique: Create fear of missing out to force action
Examples:
"Limited time offer"
"Only 3 left in stock"
"Price increases tomorrow"
Why it works: Loss aversion is stronger than desire for gain
6. COMMITMENT & CONSISTENCY
Technique: Get small commitments first, then escalate
Examples:
Free trial → paid subscription
Small favor → larger request
Public pledge → pressure to follow through
Why it works: We want to appear consistent in our behavior
7. LIKING PRINCIPLE
Technique: Make yourself likable to increase compliance
Examples:
Compliments and flattery
Finding common interests
Physical attractiveness
Why it works: We're more likely to say yes to people we like
8. EMOTIONAL MANIPULATION
Technique: Trigger emotions to override rational thinking
Examples:
Fear-based marketing
Guilt-tripping
Hope selling
Why it works: Emotions short-circuit logical analysis
INTERPERSONAL MANIPULATION TACTICS
9. GASLIGHTING
Technique: Make someone doubt their own perception
Examples:
"You're too sensitive"
"That never happened"
"You're remembering it wrong"
Why it works: Creates self-doubt and dependency
10. LOVE BOMBING
Technique: Overwhelming attention and affection to create dependency
Examples:
Constant praise and attention
Excessive gifts and compliments
"You're perfect" statements
Why it works: Creates emotional addiction
11. SILENT TREATMENT
Technique: Withdraw attention to punish and control
Examples:
Ignoring texts/calls
Giving cold shoulder
Refusing to acknowledge presence
Why it works: Creates anxiety and desire for approval
12. TRIANGULATION
Technique: Bring third parties into conflicts to control dynamics
Examples:
"Your friend agrees with me"
Comparing to others
Creating jealousy
Why it works: Divides attention and creates insecurity
BUSINESS & SALES MANIPULATION
13. BAIT AND SWITCH
Technique: Attract with one offer, then switch to another
Examples:
"Sale ended, but here's a similar deal"
Job offer with different terms
Product unavailable, upsell to expensive version
Why it works: Momentum and sunk cost fallacy
14. FALSE COMPARISON
Technique: Compare to inferior options to make yours seem better
Examples:
"Better than our competitor's old model"
Comparing to straw man arguments
Cherry-picking favorable metrics
Why it works: Relative value perception
15. ANCHORING
Technique: Set a reference point to influence subsequent judgments
Examples:
High initial price → discount seems good
First number mentioned in negotiation
"Originally $1000, now $300"
Why it works: First information heavily influences decisions
16. FOOT-IN-THE-DOOR
Technique: Start with small request, then escalate
Examples:
"Can I have 5 minutes?" → hour-long presentation
Small purchase → upsell to premium
Minor commitment → major investment
Why it works: Consistency principle and momentum
PSYCHOLOGICAL MANIPULATION
17. COGNITIVE DISSONANCE EXPLOITATION
Technique: Create mental discomfort to force behavior change
Examples:
Making someone justify their choices
Highlighting hypocrisy
Creating internal conflict
Why it works: People resolve discomfort by changing beliefs/behavior
18. CONFIRMATION BIAS USE
Technique: Present information that confirms existing beliefs
Examples:
Telling people what they want to hear
Echo chambers
Selective evidence presentation
Why it works: We seek information that confirms our views
19. DUNNING-KRUGER EFFECT EXPLOITATION
Technique: Target overconfidence in incompetence
Examples:
"You're smart enough to see this opportunity"
Flattering ignorance as insight
Simplifying complex topics
Why it works: Incompetent people overestimate their ability
20. FEAR OF MISSING OUT (FOMO)
Technique: Highlight others' success to create anxiety
Examples:
"Everyone else is getting rich"
Social media success stories
Limited opportunity messaging
Why it works: Social comparison and loss aversion
DEFENSIVE AWARENESS
HOW TO SPOT MANIPULATION
Pressure tactics: Urgency, scarcity, threats
Emotional triggers: Fear, guilt, hope, shame
Information control: One-sided stories, missing context
Social pressure: Group dynamics, comparisons
Authority plays: Uncited expertise, credentials
Reciprocity traps: Unsolicited favors, gifts
COUNTER-MEASURES
Slow down: Manipulation needs speed
Verify claims: Check sources and evidence
Consult others: Outside perspective breaks isolation
Notice emotions: Strong feelings indicate manipulation
Ask questions: Force specifics and accountability
Set boundaries: Learn to say "no" comfortably
These basic tactics combine and evolve into the more sophisticated techniques you've seen in advanced materials. Master these fundamentals and you'll recognize manipulation in any context.
