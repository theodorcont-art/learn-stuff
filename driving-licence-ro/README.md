## The exam (checked 9 Oct 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| Questions | 26 |
| Time | 30 minutes |
| To pass | at least 22 correct (max 4 wrong) |
| Answers | 3 options (A, B, C); one, two or all three can be correct |
| Retake after a fail | at least 15 days later |
| Minimum age | 18 |

A question only counts as right if you tick every correct letter and nothing else.
You can push a question to the end ("Răspunde mai târziu").

Sources: [avocatnet.ro, Sept 2026](https://www.avocatnet.ro/articol_72635/Permis-auto-Cum-se-poate-ob%C8%9Bine-in-2026-categoria-B.html),
[scoalarutiera.ro quiz format](https://www.scoalarutiera.ro/chestionare-drpciv-categoria-b).

## Plan (about one chapter every 3 to 5 days)

Order follows the traffic code (OUG 195/2002) and the
[scoalarutiera.ro legislation course](https://www.scoalarutiera.ro/curs-legislatie/codul-rutier).

1. Drumul public: road anatomy and definitions — [notes](notes/01-drumul-public.md)
2. Deplasarea pe drum: lane use, keeping right, following distance
3. Indicatoare rutiere: road signs by family
4. Marcaje, semafoare, polițist, semnale speciale
5. Prioritatea (extra time, most questions)
6. Intersecții, sens giratoriu, sens unic, autostradă, zone rezidențiale
7. Viteza
8. Manevre: depășire, oprire, staționare, parcare, întoarcere, mers înapoi
9. Locuri speciale: cale ferată, treceri pietoni, curbe, rampe, tuneluri
10. Obligațiile șoferului: before/during trip, children, accident, breakdown, first year
11. Sancțiuni: points, fines, alcohol, offences
12. Prim ajutor, mecanică, conducere ecologică și preventivă
13. Mock exams (26 questions, 30 minutes)
